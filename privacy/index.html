Privacy

Your QR data stays on your device.

The QR generator runs in your browser. The link, Wi-Fi details, contact details, logo, and downloaded QR file you use in the tool are not sent to this site.

The short version
  • No account is required to make a QR code.
  • The generator does not upload your QR content or logo.
  • The site uses privacy-friendly, cookie-free Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate page and performance measurement.
  • Links to other companies, including print shops, PayPal, and retailers, follow their own privacy practices.

What the generator does

When you enter a website address, phone number, Wi-Fi network, contact card, or other QR destination, the browser turns that information into a QR pattern locally. Your QR content is used to render the preview and download on your device. It is not stored by QR Codes Made Easy, and it is not sent to a QR redirect service.

Site measurement

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate page views and site performance. Cloudflare describes this product as cookie-free analytics. We use it to keep the site working well, not to build profiles of visitors or follow you around the web.

Cookies and advertising

This site does not use advertising trackers or an account system. It does not use QR scans to identify you, because static QR codes generated here do not pass through this site after you download them. The hosting and security services used to deliver a website may process technical request information as described in their own policies.

External links

Some pages link to services such as printers, display retailers, PayPal, or social and messaging platforms. If you follow one of those links, that other site handles any information you provide there under its own privacy policy. Some product links may become affiliate links; that does not change the price you pay or how the QR generator works.

Changes to this page

If the way this site handles data changes, this page will be updated to match. Last updated: August 9, 2026.
